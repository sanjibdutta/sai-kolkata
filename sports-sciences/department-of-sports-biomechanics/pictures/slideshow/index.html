Department overview

Department of Sports Biomechanics was introduced in the year of 1983 as a part of Faculty of Sports Sciences, NSNIS Kolkata. Since then, the department is working towards providing biomechanical support to national and international level athletes as well as educating coaches & athletes regarding importance of biomechanics and correct technique in sports.

Main focus

  • To provide scientific support to National camper, NCOE athletes, Khelo India athletes, athletes under TOPS scheme and STC athletes.
  • To identify the reason for recurrent injuries and suggest intervention in coordination with Physiotherapy and SnC department.
  • To educate coaches and athletes regarding importance of understanding biomechanics in sports.
  • To prepare Biomechanics education material for awareness purpose.
  • To conduct research in the area of sports biomechanics.
  • To provide support to various academic courses conducted by NSNIS, Kolkata.

Testing parameters

Speed and agility test using electronic timing gates
  • Speed test
  • Split timings
  • Lap timings
  • Agility test
Video analysis
  • Qualitative analysis
  • Quantitative analysis
  • Kinematic parameters
  • Kinesiological analysis
Force profile
  • Countermovement jump
  • Drop jump
  • Balance test
  • Any specific test on need basis
Isokinetic dynamometer
  • Torque parameter
  • Position parameter
  • Time parameter
  • Asymmetry
  • Agonist-Antagonist ratio

Equipment

  • Force Platform with Bioware software (Kistler -9281 EA, 600mm x 400 mm)
  • Portable force platform with MARS software (Kistler – 9286 BA, 600mm x 400mm)
  • Video Analysis System, consist of 3 high speed camera (Sony FDR AX 700) and Dartfish Pro(s) video analysis software
  • Electronic Timing gates (smart speed)
  • Isokinetic Dynamometer (Humac Norm)
