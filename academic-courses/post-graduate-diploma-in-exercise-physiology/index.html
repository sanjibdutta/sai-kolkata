Program Overview

The course is primarily designed to understand how the body reacts and adapts to the regular structured exercise, which play a key role to improving health and athletic performance. This course is designed to equip healthcare and Physiologist professionals with key insights into the science behind exercise. This course can facilitate knowledge in Exercise Prescription programs for Sports Persons /General clients. Over the course, students will understand the impact of exercise and physical activity on Human Body, and how Physiology can be a part of the Process of High-performance in the Sports Ecosystem. Conducted by various National & International Faculties.

Programme Modules

Semester 1

  • Systemic Physiology-I
  • Systemic Physiology-II
  • Sports Nutrition & Biochemistry
  • Strength and Conditioning
  • Integrated Nutrition Biochemistry (Practical)
  • Strength and Conditioning(Practical)
  • Project

Semester 2

  • Physiological aspects of Health & Fitness and Biostatistics
  • Physiological Assessment & Principles of Exercise Prescription
  • Implementation of Exercise Prescription
  • Environmental Physiology
  • Field & Lab Practical Physiology-I
  • Field & Lab Practical Physiology-II
  • Internship

Pedagogy

Pedagogy is highly interactive and will consist of a judicious blend of lectures, real life case studies, computer simulations, case presentations, quizzes and assignments
