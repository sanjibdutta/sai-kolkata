Program Overview

The Faculty of Sports Sciences which was established in 1983 at this Institute is organizing every year a two year Post Graduate Diploma in Sports Medicine for MBBS Doctors.This Diploma is affiliated with Baba Farid University of Health Sciences, Faridkot ( India ) and is recognized by the National Medical Commission.

METHOD OF INSTRUCTION

Theory

  • Through lectures, clinical discussions, seminars, case presentations, assignments.
  • ATTENDANCE Students of DSM Course require minimum of 85% attendance.
  • MEDIUM English only

Practical

  • Rotatory postings in Govt. Medical College , Kolkata in Medicine, Orthopaedics, Surgery and Anaesthesia. Providing medical cover to sports persons during National Coaching Camps, National level competitions and attending to sports persons injured during training at the Institute apart from conducting various sport scientific laboratory tests for evaluation of the functional and reserve capacities of sportspersons.

PRIVATE PRACTICE

Private practice, or employment in any other hospital, by the students, during the PGDSM tenure, is strictly prohibited.
